Primary markets: Missoula urban core, Hip Strip (S Higgins Ave),
Reserve Street corridor, Brooks Street retail, Missoula County
including Lolo, Florence, Stevensville.

Key demand drivers:
- University of Montana (largest employer, 12,000+ students)
- St. Patrick Hospital, Community Medical Center
- Limited commercial inventory in core corridors
- Strong owner-user and regional investor demand
- Seasonal tourism and outdoor recreation economy

Market tone (2025-2026):
- Retail investment market stable, continued demand for
  well-located assets
- Higher interest rates moderated some investor activity
  but not killed demand
- Hip Strip and downtown assets command premium pricing
- Industrial and flex seeing strong demand, limited supply
- Office market softer, owner-user deals more active
